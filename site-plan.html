Individual Project Site Plan

Site Name

NatureLens Photography

"NatureLens Photography" was chosen to reflect a professional focus on nature, wildlife, and scenic photography. It is simple, clear, and suitable for branding.
Optional domain: naturelensphoto.com

Site Purpose

This website serves as a professional photography portfolio for showcasing nature and wildlife photography. It offers an online gallery, blog articles on photography techniques, and a contact form for client inquiries, collaborations, and print purchases.

Scenarios

  • Where can I view your best nature and wildlife photography?
  • How do I get in touch to book a photo session or order a print?

Color Scheme

A clean, professional color palette:

  • #1F3A93 – Primary color (used in headings, navbar, footer)
  • #F4F4F4 – Secondary/background color
  • #1ABC9C – Accent color (buttons, links, hover effects)

Typography

  • Lora (Serif): Used for all headings for a clean, elegant look.
  • Open Sans (Sans-serif): Used for all body text to ensure readability and professionalism.
